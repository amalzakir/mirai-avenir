Competitive Edge

Why MIRAI?

Explore how MIRAI outperforms standard phone applications and why customers choose smart mirrors.

While phones offer beauty apps, MIRAI uniquely combines colour analysis, makeup guidance, hairstyle suggestions, and outfit recommendations into one dedicated physical mirror. Its built-in camera, sensors, and controlled lighting provide much more consistent analysis than variable phone setups.
  • 💰 Save money: Explore different looks before spending money on cosmetics or clothes that may not suit them.
  • ⏱️ Save time: Get personalized styling suggestions in one single place instead of switching across multiple apps.
  • 🛍️ Shop smarter: Discover products suited to their exact preferences through our brand partnerships.
MIRAI costs more upfront than free mobile apps and requires physical space. To overcome this, we target salons and beauty stores first, where one kiosk can serve many customers and demonstrate value!
